FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is SIMBOX?
SIMBOX is a complete, plug-in racing or flight simulator built into a concert-grade flight case. Wheel, pedals, racing seat, curved display, gaming PC and sound system are pre-assembled and pre-cabled — you plug in the power and play. It's designed for trade fairs, brand activations, product launches and sim racing events.
Which SIMBOX models are available?
Four models: SIMBOX Rally (plug-in rally cockpit), SIMBOX F1 (formula-style cockpit), SIMBOX Motion (full-motion racing platform on a 6-DOF motion base) and SIMBOX Flight (full-motion flight simulator with HOTAS controls). Every configuration is fully customizable — components can be swapped or upgraded to match your event and budget.
How long does setup take at an event?
Minutes, not hours. The simulator ships assembled inside its case — there is no cabling and no configuration on site. Open the case, plug in a single power connection and the system is ready to run.
How is SIMBOX transported?
The closed case fits a standard L2H1 delivery van and rolls on a removable dolly with skids. Aluminium fittings, ball corners and internal protective foam make it safe to transport by road, rail or air — it's built to the same standards as touring concert equipment.
Can SIMBOX be branded with my company's identity?
Yes. We offer full custom branding: vinyl wraps of the case and rig, branded boot screens and in-game overlays, leaderboards with your logo, custom color accents and laser-engraved panels — all designed to your brand book.
Is financing or leasing available?
Yes. In partnership with EFL (Crédit Agricole Group) we offer leasing and installment plans with terms tailored to your business, so you can spread the cost while the simulator earns rental revenue.
Can SIMBOX earn money as rental inventory?
Many owners rent their SIMBOX to event agencies and brands. Market day rates for event simulators typically range from several hundred to a few thousand euro per event day, depending on the model and market — our on-page calculator lets you estimate break-even for your own pricing.
